Healthcare coordination, built around real user roles.

HealHub is a multi-role healthcare prototype connecting patients and doctors across clinics through scheduling, prescriptions, community features, ratings, and notifications — designed as a complete product flow rather than a collection of isolated screens.

Explore the product View repository

Two roles. One coordinated experience.

The prototype treats patient and doctor journeys as connected workflows. Scheduling, prescriptions, community activity, ratings, and notifications share the same product context instead of existing as disconnected features.

Patient experience

Discover doctors, inspect profiles, book against available schedules, follow appointment status, read prescriptions, rate care, and participate in the community.

Doctor experience

Configure specialty and weekly availability, review appointments, manage confirmed visits, issue prescriptions, receive notifications, and participate in community content.

Shared coordination

Role-aware state and Firebase-backed flows keep the two experiences connected around the same appointment and communication lifecycle.

Engineering focus

HealHub was built as a working portfolio prototype with attention to application structure, reactive state, Firebase integration, scheduling logic, and clear separation between product functionality and production-security requirements.

Role-aware architecture

Flutter screens and routing adapt to patient and doctor responsibilities while GetX coordinates controllers, dependencies, and reactive state.

Schedule-aware booking

Doctor working hours and appointment lifecycle logic form the basis for availability and booking rather than treating appointments as static records.

Production-aware scope

The showcase explicitly separates prototype behavior from production authorization, App Check, hardened Firebase rules, compliance, and infrastructure controls.
